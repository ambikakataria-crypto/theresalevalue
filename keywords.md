## Placement rules

1. **Homepage title / H1**: lead with `car value` or `car valuation` (33K head term).
2. **/valuation page title / H1**: lead with `used car valuation` or `car value calculator` (tool intent).
3. **/valuation page body**: work in `car value check`, `vehicle value calculator`, `used car price calculator` naturally.
4. **Model pages**: `{model} resale value`, `{model} car value`, `{model} used car value`.
5. **Insights posts**: `car resale value`, `used car value`, `used car price estimator` in body + FAQ where topical.
6. **Meta descriptions**: pair one head + one tool-intent term (e.g., `used car valuation` + `car value calculator`).
7. **Alt text on images**: descriptive first, KW-adjacent only where honest.
8. **No stuffing**. If a sentence reads unnatural after inserting the KW, cut it.
9. **India-friendly phrasing** wins over KW-perfect phrasing. "Check car valuation" beats "car value online India" as a CTA.
